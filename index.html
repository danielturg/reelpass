Reelpass

Send Instagram reels and X posts to people on WhatsApp who don't have those apps.

1 · Add it to your share menu

  1. Open this page in Chrome and install it: menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install. installed ✓
  2. On a reel or post: Share → Share to… (or More) → Reelpass.
  3. Tap a person. WhatsApp opens on their chat with the link ready, then hit send.

iPhones don't let websites into the share menu, so a tiny Shortcut does it (one time).

  2. Tap ⓘ → turn on Show in Share Sheet. Set it to receive URLs and Text.
